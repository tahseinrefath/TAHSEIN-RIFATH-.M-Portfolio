About Myself

My name is Tahsein Refath M, and I recently completed my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science with Data Science. Somewhere between my first "Hello World" and my final semester project, programming stopped being coursework and became something I actually enjoy doing.

I've worked with Python, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Excel and Tableau — and I like putting them together to build things people can actually use. My final year project, the Nehru Canteen Management System, let me take a real campus problem (long food queues) and solve it with a working web app: online ordering, digital tokens, and live order tracking.

I also completed a short internship in R Programming with Data Science, where I got hands-on with data cleaning, visualization and statistical analysis using dplyr, ggplot2 and tidyr.

I'm a quick learner and I like being handed a problem I don't immediately know how to solve — that's usually where I learn the most. As a fresher, I'm looking for a team where I can keep building that way: taking on real problems, learning from people ahead of me, and contributing wherever I can.
